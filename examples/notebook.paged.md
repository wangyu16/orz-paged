## Tuesday — titration

Standardised the sodium hydroxide solution against potassium hydrogen phthalate
(KHP). Three trials, each weighed to the nearest 0.1 mg, dissolved in about 50 mL
of water with two drops of phenolphthalein, titrated to the first faint pink that
lasted thirty seconds.

| Trial | KHP (g) | NaOH (mL) | c (mol/L) |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 0.5104 | 24.85 | 0.1006 |
| 2 | 0.5098 | 24.80 | 0.1007 |
| 3 | 0.5110 | 24.92 | 0.1004 |

The concentration follows from the moles of KHP over the volume of base,

$$c_{\text{NaOH}} = \frac{m_{\text{KHP}}}{M_{\text{KHP}}\,V_{\text{NaOH}}}, \qquad M_{\text{KHP}} = 204.22\ \text{g/mol}$$

and the three trials agree to within 0.3 %. The mean, 0.1006 mol/L, goes on the
bottle label with today's date.

::: warning
**Careful.** Sodium hydroxide absorbs carbon dioxide from the air: keep the
bottle closed and standardise again after a week.
:::

## Next steps
